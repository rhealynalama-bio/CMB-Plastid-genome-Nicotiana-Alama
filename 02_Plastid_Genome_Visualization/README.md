**Scientific name:** *Nicotiana tabacum* (common tobacco)
**NCBI accession:** NC_001879.2
**Plastid genome length:** 155,943 bp
**Source of genome file:** NCBI Nucleotide (RefSeq), annotated GenBank format
**Software used:** OGDRAW (OrganellarGenomeDRAW) v1.3.1, https://chlorobox.mpimp-golm.mpg.de/OGDraw.html

## OGDRAW settings
Standard map mode; GenBank file uploaded; circular map; plastid as the
sequence source; inverted repeats detected automatically; GC content graph,
direction of transcription, full legend and intron asterisks enabled; PNG
output.

## Main structural features
The *N. tabacum* plastome has the typical quadripartite structure: a large
single-copy region (LSC, 86,686 bp) and a small single-copy region (SSC,
18,571 bp) separated by two inverted repeats, IRb and IRa (25,343 bp each).
Genes in the IRs, including the rRNA genes, rpl2, rpl23, trnI, ndhB, rps7,
rps12 and ycf2, appear twice on the map. The LSC contains photosystem I and
II genes (psaA, psaB, psbA), rbcL and the RNA polymerase genes, while the SSC
contains ndhF, ndhD and ccsA. Genes are drawn on both the outer and inner
sides of the ring, so they are transcribed from both strands. Intron-containing
genes are marked with an asterisk (e.g., rpl2*, ndhB*, petB*). The GC content
graph varies among regions rather than being uniform.
