## Plastome summary
Circular plastid genome of 155,943 bp with 37.85% GC content. It has the
typical LSC-IR-SSC-IR arrangement: LSC 86,686 bp, IR 25,343 bp (two copies)
and SSC 18,571 bp.

## Download and Galaxy upload
The annotated record was found by searching NCBI Nucleotide, and the FASTA
and GenBank files were downloaded from the record page. The FASTA file was
uploaded to usegalaxy.org and detected as fasta.

## Gene content and observations
The GenBank annotation lists 144 gene features (98 CDS, 37 tRNA, 8 rRNA),
counting genes duplicated in the IR twice. The rRNA genes (16S, 23S, 4.5S
and 5S) occur in both IR copies. Intron-containing genes include atpF,
clpP, ndhA, ndhB, petB, petD, rpl16, rpl2, rpoC1, rps12, rps16 and ycf3.
Galaxy confirmed a single sequence record with no Ns.
